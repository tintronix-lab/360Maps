Sweden on one map, in real time.

360Maps is made for visiting Sweden. Find the must-see sights, ride the buses, trains and boats, see traffic, weather and parking, and get spoken directions, all in English on iPhone. The map above is Stockholm right now, drawn with the same data as the app.

What you see on the map

Switch on what you need. Everything comes from open data from public authorities and transport operators, and is updated while you look.

  • Sights

    Over 300 must-see places in Sweden, with a photo and a short text in English. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and texts from Wikipedia.

  • 41441

    Buses, metro, trains and boats

    Vehicles move on the map with their line number, in 15 counties. Red is bus, blue metro, purple train and turquoise boat.

  • Queues and travel times

    Measured travel times on Stockholm's main roads and tunnels, every minute. A queue shows as +2 minutes compared with normal.

  • Road conditions

    Snow, slush and ice on the roads as lines, with what is on the road and when it was ploughed.

  • Rain radar

    Current precipitation from SMHI's weather radar, every five minutes. Blue is light, yellow and red heavy.

  • Disruptions and roadworks

    Accidents, closures and roadworks from the Swedish Transport Administration, with place and time.

  • Traffic cameras

    Tap a camera to see the road. The picture refreshes by itself while you look.

  • Ferries and ships

    The next departure for the Swedish Transport Administration's road ferries, and ships moving at sea.

  • Parking at your destination

    When you plan a trip by car, see parking near where you are going, with the number of spaces and the price. Free spaces show where the city shares them.

  • Traffic lights on the road

    Where the next traffic light is when you drive. Colour and countdown will come when cities share their signal data openly.

  • Police, Krisinformation and SMHI

    Incidents where you are, important public announcements and weather warnings.

From A to B

  • Drive
  • Transit
  • Cycle
  • Walk

Choose how you want to travel. 360Maps compares the routes with the traffic right now and shows what is along your way: queues, road conditions, roadworks, ferries and traffic lights. By public transport you get departures, delays and changes across Sweden.

On a trip by car, bike or on foot the next turn is read aloud, even with the screen locked. If you take a wrong turn the app finds a new route, and if it finds one that is at least three minutes faster it tells you. Near your destination you see where to park.

A day out

Surprise me

The app plans a day from where you are: sights and parks, with lunch and coffee at places that are open. Choose walking, bike or public transport, and shuffle until the day suits you.

Museums, palaces and churches are placed before 17:00, because the app does not know their opening hours.

Catching a bus or a train? Tap the departure and choose Get me there in time, and the app tells you when to leave.

Free and 360Maps Plus

The map with all its layers, search, public transport, bike, walking, Surprise me and planning a car route are free. Your first three trips with car guidance are free too.

With 360Maps Plus you get car guidance: turn by turn read aloud, warnings about queues, ice and roadworks, and a new route when traffic changes. And this:

What the trip costs, and a winter check

Congestion tax and bridge tolls along your car route, worked out for when you pass, and what it costs if you leave later.

From 1 November to 30 April: ice and snow along the way, the temperature where you will be when you get there, and SMHI's warnings.

Parking rules in Stockholm

Zoom in and the kerbs are coloured: green where you may park, orange where the street is cleaned within a day, red where parking is banned now and grey where the place is reserved.

According to the city's regulations. Signs on the street apply.

Where the car stands

Save where the car is, and the app shows what applies right there: the price now, the full tariff and when the street is cleaned.

Remind me gives a notification the evening before and an hour before street cleaning starts.
