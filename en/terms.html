What is free

The map with all its layers, search, public transport journeys, bike and walking directions, and planning a car route are free. So are your first three trips with car guidance. A trip counts once you have driven 500 metres with guidance on.

360Maps Plus

360Maps Plus is the app’s own guidance in the car: turn by turn read aloud, warnings about queues, ice and roadworks along your route, a new route when traffic changes, and parking at your destination. You can buy it in four ways:

  • One week. Lasts seven days from the purchase and ends by itself. It is not a subscription and does not renew.
  • Month. A subscription that renews every month until you cancel.
  • Year. A subscription that renews every year until you cancel.
  • One-time purchase. You pay once and keep Plus. It is not a subscription and never renews.

The price is shown in the app before you buy, in your own currency. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. You manage and cancel it under Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your phone, or under About 360Maps in the app. Refunds are handled by Apple.

A purchase follows your Apple Account. “Restore purchases” in the app brings it back on a new phone or after reinstalling.
