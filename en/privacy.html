360Maps

Privacy policy

Effective from 25 November 2026.

360Maps shows traffic, public transport, weather and disruptions on a map, and helps you find sights, parking and your way around Sweden. The app has no ads, no account and no tracking. This is what happens to your data.

Your location

If you give the app access to your location, it is used while the app is open, to show what is near you and to plan journeys from there. During guidance it is also used when the app is in the background or the screen is locked, so that you hear the next turn. That stops when you arrive or end the trip. The same goes while you record a journey or share your location live, until you stop. Your location is never linked to you, and we don’t store it. The one exception is live sharing, where it is kept with us in encrypted form while the sharing lasts (see below).

What is sent, and where

  • Our server receives the visible area of the map when you look at vehicles and other dense layers, which stop you open, and the start and destination when you plan a public transport journey. It uses this to answer and does not store it.
  • Apple receives the start and destination when you plan a journey by car, bike or on foot, and draws the map. Apple’s terms apply to that.
  • The data sources, for example SL and SMHI, receive requests about the area you are looking at, with no information about who you are.
  • Our server is asked, during guidance by car, for the speed limits in the kilometre square you are driving in. The server receives only which square it is, not where in it you are or who you are, and does not store it.
  • Photos of sights come from Wikimedia Commons. Our server fetches the photo from Wikimedia for the app, so Wikimedia never sees you. The server receives which photo is shown, uses this to answer and does not store it.
  • Water temperature and northern lights: the app fetches the day's water-temperature forecast for all bathing places from the Swedish Agency for Marine and Water Management, and the northern-lights forecast for the whole world from NOAA in the USA. Neither receives where you are.
  • Look Around: when you open a sight, the app asks Apple for street-level imagery at the sight's position, not yours. Apple's terms apply to that.

Sharing your location

“Send location” puts your address and a link with directions to you in a message, and you choose who it goes to. It doesn’t go through us.

“Share live” shows where you are to anyone with the link, until they arrive, the time you chose runs out or you tap Stop. While sharing, the app sends your location to our server when you have moved about 25 metres, and once a minute when you stand still, also when the app is in the background. The location and address, and your name if you have typed one in, are encrypted on your phone with a key that only exists in the link. The name is otherwise kept on your phone only; there is no account. Browsers never send that part of a link, so our server cannot read where you are. The server only holds the latest encrypted location and when the sharing ends, and deletes everything at the latest ten minutes after the sharing has ended. Anyone with the link can see where you are while the sharing lasts, so only send it to people you want to see that.

The person who opens the link can choose to see how far away you are. The distance is then worked out in their browser and sent nowhere.

Restaurants, cafés and table booking

The restaurants, cafés and shopping centres come with the app, so browsing them on the map and in the lists sends nothing. When you tap “Ratings & photos”, the app asks Apple Maps about that place, and Apple’s terms apply. A booking page belongs to the restaurant’s booking provider, and their privacy policy applies there. 360Maps sends no information about which places you look at.

What is saved on your phone

The app remembers your choices, such as which map layers are on and which notices you have dismissed, your recent destinations, the places you have saved as Home and Work, and where the phone was last (so the map opens over the right area). They stay on your phone only, are never sent to us, and disappear if you delete the app. You remove a destination by pressing and holding it in the list. For places where you have opened “Ratings & photos”, the phone remembers Apple Maps’ identifier for that place, so the lookup runs only once. It stays on your phone and is never sent to us. The history of places whose card you opened and trips you followed, the places you marked as visited and the days you plan are kept on the phone in the same way; you clear the history under History → Clear history.

Purchases

360Maps Plus is bought through Apple. The app and our server never receive your card or other payment details, and we keep no record of what you have bought. The phone asks Apple whether you have Plus. How many of your three free car trips you have used is saved on the phone only, in its keychain, so the number is still there if you reinstall the app. It is never sent to us.

Technical logs

Our server keeps technical logs for a short time so that we can fix errors. They contain which parts of the service were requested, not who you are, and are cleared automatically.

This website

The website uses no cookies and no analytics service. The fonts are hosted on the website itself. The live map on the front page fetches vehicle positions from the same server as the app. A copy of the website is hosted on GitHub Pages (tintronix-lab.github.io/360Maps), where GitHub keeps technical logs under its own terms.
