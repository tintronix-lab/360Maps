Privacy policy

Effective from 21 November 2026.

360Maps shows traffic, public transport, weather and disruptions on a map, and helps you find sights, parking and your way around Sweden. The app has no ads, no account and no tracking. This is what happens to your data.

Your location

If you give the app access to your location, it is used while the app is open, to show what is near you and to plan journeys from there. During guidance it is also used when the app is in the background or the screen is locked, so that you hear the next turn. That stops when you arrive or end the trip. Your location is not stored and is never linked to you.

What is sent, and where

  • Our server receives the visible area of the map when you look at vehicles and other dense layers, which stop you open, and the start and destination when you plan a public transport journey. It uses this to answer and does not store it.
  • Apple receives the start and destination when you plan a journey by car, bike or on foot, and draws the map. Apple's terms apply to that.
  • The data sources, for example SL and SMHI, receive requests about the area you are looking at, with no information about who you are.
  • Photos of sights come from Wikimedia Commons. Our server fetches the photo from Wikimedia for the app, so Wikimedia never sees you. The server receives which photo is shown, uses this to answer and does not store it.

What is saved on your phone

The app remembers your choices, such as which map layers are on and which notices you have dismissed, your recent destinations and the places you have saved as Home and Work. They stay on your phone only, are never sent to us, and disappear if you delete the app. You remove a destination by pressing and holding it in the list.

Technical logs

Our server keeps technical logs for a short time so that we can fix errors. They contain which parts of the service were requested, not who you are, and are cleared automatically.

This website

The website uses no cookies and no analytics service. The fonts are hosted on the website itself. The live map on the front page fetches vehicle positions from the same server as the app. A copy of the website is hosted on GitHub Pages (tintronix-lab.github.io/360Maps), where GitHub keeps technical logs under its own terms.

Questions

If you have questions about privacy or how the app handles your data, email raztechsupport@icloud.com.
