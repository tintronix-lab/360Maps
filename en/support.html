The map is empty where I am

360Maps shows data for Sweden. Moving vehicles appear where the transport operator shares its positions openly, today in 15 counties.

A layer is missing or shows nothing

Tap the layers button at the top right and switch on what you want to see. If a source does not answer at that moment, the app tries again shortly.

Sights

The Sights layer shows over 300 must-see places in Sweden. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and the descriptions from Wikipedia, each with its credit on the card. Tap a sight to read more or to plan a trip there.

Voice guidance

On a journey by car, bike or on foot, the next turn is read aloud, even with the screen locked. The speaker button in the banner at the top turns the voice off and on.

Parking at your destination

The number of spaces and the price come from OpenStreetMap, and free spaces from the cities that share them openly. If something is missing, it is usually missing in the source too.

Home, Work and recent destinations

Press and hold a destination in the list to save it as Home or Work, or to remove it. Your destinations are saved only on your phone.

Language

The app speaks English, Swedish, German, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Dutch, French, Spanish and Italian, and follows the language your iPhone is set to. You can change it for 360Maps alone under Settings, 360Maps, Language.

Location services

The app needs your location to show what is near you and to plan journeys from there. You change it under Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, 360Maps. Read more in the privacy policy.
